Privacy Policy

Free Robux Rewards
Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Free Robux Rewards ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

Important: Free Robux Rewards is an independent app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Roblox Corporation. Roblox is a trademark of Roblox Corporation.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, we may collect or process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Firebase user ID, sign-in provider, and email address if you create or link an account using Google or email/password.
  • Profile information: nickname/display name, avatar selection, profile visibility, and similar profile preferences.
  • Reward and gameplay information: Coin balance, XP, level, streaks, mission progress, game sessions, game results, reward claims, and related timestamps.
  • Redemption information: reward tier, Coin cost, RBX amount, redemption status, payout batch, and fulfillment history. If you voluntarily provide a username or other payout identifier for manual fulfillment, we use it only for reward delivery, support, fraud prevention, and recordkeeping.
  • Device and app information: app version, Firebase Cloud Messaging token, installation-related identifiers needed for notifications, App Check/Play Integrity signals, and technical diagnostics.
  • Advertising information: ad impressions, rewarded-ad claims, AdMob transaction identifiers, consent signals, and other information processed by Google Mobile Ads/AdMob.
  • Integrity and abuse-prevention information: suspicious activity indicators, impossible game-result attempts, duplicate reward attempts, redemption-risk indicators, and related security events.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: crash, performance, and analytics information when those services are enabled and permitted by your consent/settings.
  • Support information: information you send to us when contacting support.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Some preferences may be stored locally on your device, such as theme, language, sound/haptics, reminder preferences, cached profile information, and temporary game state. Locally stored information may be removed when you clear app data or uninstall the App.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and maintain your account;
  • provide game, mission, streak, Coin, XP, and reward features;
  • validate reward eligibility and prevent duplicate or fraudulent reward claims;
  • process and manually fulfill redemption requests;
  • send account, reward, and status notifications when enabled;
  • show and measure advertising;
  • provide customer support;
  • protect the App, our users, and our reward economy from abuse;
  • diagnose crashes, performance problems, and technical errors; and
  • comply with legal and platform requirements.

4. Advertising and Consent

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertising, including rewarded and interstitial ads. We do not use banner ads in the current version of the App.

Where required, we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage consent and privacy choices. If you decline personalized advertising or opt out of certain data uses, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads where permitted by applicable law and Google's advertising systems. Ads are requested only when the applicable Google consent state allows ad requests.

Rewarded ads are optional. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad does not prevent you from accessing the basic App experience. Rewarded ads may provide optional benefits such as an extra spin, extra scratch, revive, hint, retry, or other eligible bonus.

5. Rewarded Ads and Server-Side Verification

Rewarded-ad benefits are verified through server-side systems. We may process a Firebase user ID, claim identifier, placement type, AdMob transaction identifier, and verification timestamps to validate that a rewarded ad was completed and to prevent replay or duplicate rewards.

6. Firebase and Google Services

The App uses Google/Firebase services that may process information on our behalf, including:

  • Firebase Authentication;
  • Cloud Firestore;
  • Cloud Functions for Firebase;
  • Firebase App Check / Google Play Integrity;
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging;
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and Performance Monitoring where enabled;
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob; and
  • Google User Messaging Platform.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share or make information available:

  • with Google/Firebase/AdMob as service providers and advertising technology providers;
  • with service providers that help operate the App, where necessary;
  • when required by law, legal process, or government request;
  • to investigate fraud, security incidents, or abuse; or
  • as part of a business transfer, merger, acquisition, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law.

8. Reward Redemptions and Manual Fulfillment

Reward redemptions are not automatically delivered by Firebase. Redemption requests are reviewed and fulfilled manually. We use redemption information to verify eligibility, hold or refund Coins, manage payout batches, track delivery status, and maintain an audit history.

Pending, rejected, cancelled, and delivered redemption records may be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, support, dispute handling, and legal compliance for an appropriate period.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including operating the reward system, preventing abuse, resolving disputes, fulfilling redemptions, maintaining security records, and meeting legal or accounting obligations.

Temporary records such as reward claims, game sessions, device tokens, and verification records may be deleted or expired automatically after defined retention periods. Some completed redemption or audit records may be anonymized instead of deleted when retention is necessary for fraud prevention, accounting, or legal reasons.

10. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information through the account-deletion feature in the App or by contacting us at freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com.

When an account is deleted, we delete or anonymize information associated with the account except where retention is reasonably necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes. Certain active redemption requests may need to be completed, cancelled, or otherwise resolved before deletion can finish.

11. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, server-side reward validation, App Check/Play Integrity, restricted administrative access, and server-side verification for rewarded advertising.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

Users must also meet any higher minimum age required by the laws of their country or region.
