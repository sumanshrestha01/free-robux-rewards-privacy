1. Introduction

This policy explains how BuxRo: Games & Rewards (the “app”), operated by Mad Axe Studios, handles information and what choices you have. It applies to the Android app with package name com.madaxe.sohan.robloxreward.

The app includes casual mini-games, daily missions and activities such as Daily Bonus, Day Streak, Spin & Win and Lucky Scratch. Activity and reward records are used to maintain in-app Coins and process eligible requests for the rewards displayed in the app. The app also includes referral features and links to our other apps.

This policy describes data handling. It does not promise reward availability, delivery or eligibility.

For privacy questions, contact freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com .

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we and our service providers handle the following information:

  • Account information: a Firebase user ID and, if you create an account, your email address, chosen username, sign-in method and account creation time. Sign-in methods include Google or email and password. Google sign-in provides the basic account information needed for authentication.
  • Profile choices: your nickname and selected avatar.
  • Game, mission and reward activity: Coins, streaks, missions, games played, daily activity status and Coin history.
  • Redemption information: information associated with reward requests you submit.
  • Advertising information: information processed by Google and its advertising partners when ads are requested or displayed.
  • Referral information: your invitation code, the account that invited you and pseudonymous hashes of network IP addresses used for abuse prevention.
  • Notification information: a notification token when you allow notifications.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: app usage events, crash reports and performance data, subject to the app’s diagnostics settings.

The app does not request access to your contacts, precise location, photos, files, camera or microphone. It does not request your Roblox password.

3. How We Use Information
  • To authenticate users, save progress and display balances and activity history.
  • To calculate, record and limit in-app rewards on our servers and review reward requests.
  • To detect bots, duplicate requests, self-referrals and other misuse of the app.
  • To operate referral features.
  • To display weekly, monthly and all-time leaderboards. These may show top players’ display names, selected avatars and Coins earned.
  • To provide requested reminders and reward-request status notifications.
  • To display advertising and respect advertising choices.
  • To answer support requests and maintain the service.
4. Guest Accounts and Sign-in

You can begin as a guest. Guest sessions use an anonymous Firebase user ID without requiring an email address or name.

Reward requests require an account and a verified email. Supported sign-in methods include email and password or Google.

When a guest session is converted into an account, its existing progress stays with it. If you sign in to an existing account, the guest session’s available Coins are transferred to that account once by our servers. Other guest progress is not merged. An account can receive Coins from one guest session only.

5. Game, Mission and Reward Activity

Reward activity is checked and recorded by our servers. Information associated with your account includes:

  • Coin balance, Coins reserved for pending reward requests and lifetime totals.
  • Day Streak and daily mission progress.
  • Games played and Daily Bonus, Spin & Win and Lucky Scratch activity.
  • Coin credits and spending, including dates and the source of each transaction.
  • Completed game-run results, such as scores, duration and game statistics used for server checks. Individual moves and taps are not sent.
  • Reward requests, including the selected reward, Coin cost, selected country, request time and processing status.

Reward eligibility checks may include activity on seven consecutive days. Day Streak information is used for this check. The current conditions shown in the app apply.

You can cancel a pending request through Profile, then History. Its reserved Coins are returned.

We may retain abuse-prevention information, including account risk signals and review notes, to protect the reward system.

6. Referral Features

The referral feature creates an invitation code and link. Sharing uses your device’s share sheet. We do not read your contacts or receive a list of the people you share with.

When an invited person installs the app, Google Play may pass the invitation code through the Play install referrer. The invited person can also enter a code manually. We record the relationship between the referring and invited accounts.

A code can be used only during the initial eligibility period of an account, and an account can be invited once. The existing referral rules award 50 Coins after an invited friend earns 250 Coins through eligible gameplay. Check the app for current conditions.

To prevent self-referrals and duplicate claims, the system stores salted SHA-256 hashes of network IP addresses. Referrals may be rejected when network hashes match or accounts invite each other.

These hashes are pseudonymous abuse-prevention identifiers; they are not treated as anonymous data. Raw IP addresses are not stored in these referral records.

Referral records are held on our servers and are not readable by other users. Referral entries shown to you do not identify other users by their account details.

Referral records are deleted with the account, except where limited records must be retained for fraud prevention, security or other reasons described below.

7. Links to Our Other Apps

The app includes a “More apps from us” section linking to our other Google Play listings. Selecting a link opens Google Play.

The app does not send your account details to those other apps when you select a link. Installing another app does not award Coins in BuxRo.

8. Advertising and Your Choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads, also known as AdMob. Advertising formats include:

  • App open ads: ads that may appear when opening or returning to the app.
  • Interstitial ads: ads between eligible actions or screens, including after opening or finishing a game or returning to the games screen. They are not displayed during gameplay, claims, sign-in or reward-request submission.
  • Optional rewarded ads: ads selected by the user for supported features, such as continuing a game, playing again, additional daily activities or temporarily unlocking the calculator.

The existing calculator unlock lasts 30 minutes. If an ad is unavailable, the calculator and Play Again remain available without one. The app does not use banner ads.

When ads are requested or displayed, Google and its advertising partners may process device and app information, advertising identifiers where available, ad interactions and approximate location derived from IP addresses.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or manage ad personalization through your Android device’s Google settings.

Where required, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to request advertising choices before requesting ads. Ads are not requested until the consent platform allows it. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, Google may display non-personalized or limited ads.

You can review advertising choices in the app through Profile, then Privacy choices.

To verify completion of rewarded ads, the app provides Google with the app user ID and a one-time claim ID. Google sends a verification response to our servers.

Ad content is limited to a teen rating. We do not provide advertisers with your email address, name or reward history, and we do not sell personal information.

9. Analytics and Diagnostics

The app uses Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring to understand feature usage, record crashes and measure performance.

Collection is enabled by default. You can disable it through Profile, then Legal, using the “Share diagnostics” setting.

These services receive information such as event names, small numeric values, app version, device model, operating system version and crash stack traces. We do not send your nickname or email address to these tools.

10. Firebase and Google Services

The app uses the following Google services:

  • Firebase Authentication: guest, email and Google authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: account records, progress, Coin activity, reward requests and public leaderboard entries.
  • Cloud Functions: server-side activity checks, reward processing and account-related requests.
  • Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity: app and device integrity checks.
  • Firebase Remote Config: feature configuration, including advertising settings.
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring: usage, crash and performance information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: notifications, including reward-request updates, after notifications are allowed.
  • Google Sign-In: authentication when you choose Google sign-in.

Google processes information to provide these services. Information may be stored on servers outside your country.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

11. Notifications

Notifications remain off until you allow them. The app may offer reminders after your first Daily Bonus. You can also manage them through Profile, then Reminders. Android notification permission is requested when appropriate.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device, with up to three reminders a day for Daily Bonus availability, unfinished missions or an incomplete daily check-in. Reminders are skipped when the corresponding activity is done.

If the app has not been opened for a couple of days, a Daily Bonus reminder may continue once a day until you open the app, disable reminders or uninstall it. These reminders are generated locally.

When you allow notifications, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token is associated with your account for server notifications. Reminder preferences are stored on your device and with your account.

You can disable reminders in the app or disable notifications in your device settings.

12. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores preferences such as language, theme, sound, music and haptics; reminder choices; profile information and avatar choices; cached content and progress; pending game results awaiting an internet connection; and the calculator unlock expiry time.

The calculator operates on your device. Numbers entered into it are not saved or transmitted.

App data is excluded from device backups. Local information is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. The app does not store your passwords on the device.

Removing local data does not by itself delete information stored in your server account. See the account deletion section below.

13. Sharing Information

We use Google services, including Firebase, Google Sign-In, AdMob and Google’s consent platform, to operate the app. Information is processed by these providers as described above.

We may disclose information where required by law or where necessary to investigate fraud, protect users or prevent abuse.

Leaderboards display participating top players’ display names, selected avatars and Coins earned during the relevant period to other app users.

14. Data Retention

Account, profile and progress information is retained while the account exists.

Coin activity and reward-request records are retained to maintain balances, process requests and investigate abuse. Limited records may remain after account deletion where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations or resolving disputes.

Short-lived operational records, such as daily activity state and advertising claims, are removed automatically after their applicable retention period.

You can contact us for information about retention relevant to your account.

15. Data Security

Safeguards include encrypted connections, server-side reward checks, database access rules and Firebase App Check.

No storage or transmission system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your sign-in information private.

16. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information; request portability; object to or restrict processing; and withdraw consent.

Advertising choices are available through Profile, then Privacy choices. Diagnostics preferences are available through Profile, then Legal.

To make a privacy request, email freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com . We may request information needed to verify account ownership. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

17. Delete Account and Data

Users of BuxRo: Games & Rewards can request deletion of their account and associated personal data. You can request deletion without reinstalling the app.

Delete through the app

  1. Open BuxRo: Games & Rewards and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the information and confirm.

The existing deletion flow blocks deletion while a reward request is being processed. A pending request can be cancelled through Profile, then History, before retrying deletion. If this prevents deletion or you cannot access your account, use the email request method below.

Request deletion without the app

Email freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com with the subject Account Deletion Request. Include your in-app username and, where possible, send the request from the email address associated with your account. Guest users can also contact us.

Do not include passwords, Google credentials or verification codes. We may ask for additional information necessary to verify ownership.

Request account deletion by email

Information deleted

  • Account and profile information and associated sign-in.
  • App progress, Coin balance, missions and streak.
  • Public profile and leaderboard entries.
  • Notification tokens and associated device records.
  • Other Firebase records directly linked to the account.

Information that may be retained

Limited Coin activity and completed reward-request records may be retained where necessary for fraud prevention, security, legal obligations or resolving disputes, and only for as long as needed for those purposes. Records are anonymized where possible.

Google may independently retain information it processes as an advertising provider under its own policies. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete server-side account information.
