01 Introduction

This policy explains what information RBX Rewards: Robux Calculator (the "app") handles, why, and what choices you have. It describes the Android app as it works on the date above; we will update it when that changes.

The app is a casual games and rewards app: you play the available games, complete daily missions and take part in daily activities (Daily Bonus, Day Streak, Spin & Win and Lucky Scratch) to earn in-app Coins, and you can use eligible Coins to request the rewards shown in the Store.

If anything here is unclear, write to freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com.

02 Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we and our service providers handle:

  • Account information: a Firebase user ID and, if you create an account, your email address, the username you choose and how you sign in (Google, or email and password). If you sign in with Google, we receive the basic account details Google provides for sign-in. We also record when the account was created.
  • Profile choices: your nickname and selected avatar.
  • Game, mission and reward activity: Coins, XP, level, streak, missions, games played, daily reward status and your Coin history.
  • Redemption information for reward requests you make.
  • Advertising information handled by Google when ads are requested or shown (see Advertising).
  • A notification token, only if you allow notifications.
  • Technical and diagnostic information, only if diagnostics are switched on (they are off, and this version has no control to switch them on).

The app does not ask for your contacts, precise location, photos, files, camera or microphone, and it never asks for your Roblox password.

03 How We Use Information
  • To run the app: sign you in, save your progress and show your balance and history.
  • To calculate, record and limit rewards on our servers, and to review redemption requests.
  • To detect and prevent bots, duplicate claims and other misuse of the reward system.
  • To show rankings, if your profile is public.
  • To show the reminders you asked for, and to tell you when a reward request changes status.
  • To show ads and respect your ad choices.
  • To answer support requests you send us, and to keep the service working.
04 Guest Accounts and Sign-in

You can start playing as a guest. A guest session uses an anonymous Firebase user ID, with no email or name. To redeem rewards you need to create an account or sign in (email and password, or Google), with a verified email.

If you turn a guest session into an account, your existing progress stays with it. If you sign in to an account you already have, the guest session’s available Coins are moved to that account once by our servers; the guest’s other progress is not merged.

05 Game, Mission and Reward Activity

Rewards are decided and recorded by our servers, never by the app on your phone, so the following is stored with your account:

  • Your Coin balance, Coins held for pending reward requests, and lifetime totals.
  • XP, level and streak.
  • Daily mission progress and claims, games played, and Daily Bonus, Spin & Win and Lucky Scratch results.
  • Coin history: each credit or spend with its date and source (for example a game, a mission or a reward request).
  • When a game run finishes, the run’s result (for example score, duration and game statistics) so the server can check it before paying. Individual moves and taps are not sent.

Reward requests record the reward you chose, its cost, the country you selected, the time and the status (for example Pending, Approved, Delivered, Not approved or Cancelled). We may keep abuse-prevention signals about an account, such as risk scores and review notes, only to protect the reward system.

06 Advertising and Your Ad Choices

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads. Three formats are used:

  • App open ads, which may appear when you open or return to the app.
  • Interstitial ads, which may appear between screens (at most after every third screen change, and never during a game, a claim, sign-in or a reward request).
  • Rewarded ads, only as an optional choice after a game over, to continue that run once. Watching one gives no Coins by itself.

There are no banner ads.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and app information, your device’s advertising ID where available, ad interaction information and general location derived from your IP address, under Google’s own policies. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device’s Google settings.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your choices through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested, and no ad is requested until that step allows it. If you decline personalized ads, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads as its rules allow. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Profile, then Privacy choices.

To confirm that a rewarded ad was completed, the app passes your app user ID and a one-time claim ID to Google, and Google sends a confirmation to our servers. Ad content is limited to a teen rating. We do not give advertisers your email, name or reward history, and we do not sell personal information.

07 Analytics and Diagnostics

The app includes Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring, but all three ship switched off and collect data only if the diagnostics preference on your device is turned on. This version does not show a control to turn it on, so these tools are not collecting data.

08 Firebase and Google Services

The app runs on Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Authentication: guest, email and Google sign-in.
  • Cloud Firestore: stores your account, progress, Coin activity, reward requests and public ranking entries.
  • Cloud Functions: the server logic that awards and checks rewards and handles reward and account requests.
  • Firebase App Check (with Google Play Integrity): helps confirm that requests come from the genuine app on a genuine device.
  • Firebase Remote Config: switches features, such as ads, on or off.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers updates about your reward requests, only after you allow notifications.

Google Sign-In is used if you choose to sign in with Google. Google processes this data for us and may store it on servers in other countries. Google’s services have their own privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy.

09 Notifications

Notifications are off until you allow them. The app offers reminders once, after your first Daily Bonus, and you can also turn them on in Profile, then Reminders. Only then does the app ask Android for notification permission.

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device, at most three a day: in the morning when the Daily Bonus is ready, around midday if missions are unfinished, and in the evening if the day’s check-in is still open. A reminder is skipped when what it would announce is already done. They are created on your device and are not sent from our servers.

When you allow notifications, the app also creates a Firebase Cloud Messaging token and saves it with your account, so we can tell you when a reward request changes status. Whether the Daily Bonus and mission reminders are on is saved on your device and with your account. You can switch reminders off in the app, or turn notifications off in your device settings, at any time.

10 Data Stored on Your Device

The app keeps on your device: preferences (language, theme, sound, music, haptics), reminder and notification choices, profile details you entered and your avatar, cached content and progress (including a copy of the day’s missions and daily reward status, shown only while the app reconnects), and game results waiting to be sent when you are back online. This is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. The app does not keep passwords on the device.

11 Sharing

We share information only with the providers needed to run the app: Google (Firebase, Google Sign-In, AdMob and its consent platform). We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect against fraud and abuse. Rankings show your nickname, avatar and level to other players only if your profile is public.

12 Data Retention

Profile and progress information is kept while your account exists. Reward records (Coin activity and reward requests) exist so balances and requests can be checked and abuse investigated; some may be kept after an account is deleted where needed for security, fraud prevention, record-keeping or legal reasons. Short-lived technical records (for example daily state and ad claims) are removed automatically after a limited period.

13 Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, server-side reward checks, database access rules and App Check. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your sign-in details private.

14 Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict some processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time (for ads: Profile, then Privacy choices).

To exercise these rights, contact us at freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com. We may ask for details to confirm the account is yours.

15 Delete Account & Data

Users of RBX Rewards: Robux Calculator can request permanent deletion of their account and associated data.

Delete your account in the app

  • Open RBX Rewards: Robux Calculator and sign in.
  • Go to Profile.
  • Select Delete Account.
  • Confirm.

Deletion starts on our servers straight away. It is refused while a reward request is still being processed, and the app tells you so. Signing out does not delete anything.

Request deletion without the app, or as a guest

Email us with the subject line Account Deletion Request, and include your in-app username. Where possible, send it from the email address of your account so we can verify ownership.

freerbxrewards.help@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Account and profile information, and the sign-in associated with it.
  • App progress, Coin balance, missions and streak.
  • Ranking and public profile entries.
  • Notification tokens and devices.
  • Other Firebase data directly linked to the account.

What may be kept

Limited reward records (such as Coin activity and completed reward requests, anonymised where possible) may be kept where reasonably required for fraud and abuse prevention, security, legal obligations or resolving disputes, and only for as long as necessary. Deleting your account does not delete records held independently by Google as an advertising provider. Uninstalling the app does not by itself delete account data stored on our servers.

16 Children
